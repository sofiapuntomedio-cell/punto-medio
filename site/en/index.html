About me

Welcome, I'm Sofía

An explorer of dwelling, in every sense of the word.

Some time ago I learned that I am not my profession, nor my age, nor my relationship with someone else. I learned that I am who I am today, in this moment: what I do with my present, my actions and the decisions I make.

Accepting that I am constantly changing allows me to grow and to go after my goals in the way I need to, without ties or limitations.

My path began in architecture and gradually expanded into the world of holistic practices, the body, emotions and self-knowledge.

Punto Medio was originally born as a space for connection and community, and it changed along with me until it became a place where architecture, wellbeing and journeys of self-discovery meet.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Outer Space

Architecture, landscape design, interior design and corporate wellbeing.

Can architecture be approached from a holistic perspective?

Yes. At Punto Medio I see space as something that shapes the way we live, feel and relate to one another. That's why, beyond the functional, technical and aesthetic aspects of architecture, I can bring in tools such as neuroarchitecture, biophilia and Feng Shui, aiming to create spaces that support the wellbeing of the people who live in them. Each proposal is adapted to the project and to each person's needs.

Can the services be done online?

Yes. Some stages of a project can be done entirely online, especially consultations, design, interior design or guidance on an existing space. For architecture or landscape projects that require an on-site survey, some steps will need to happen in person. Before we begin, we'll decide together which format works best for your project.

Can I combine different services?

Yes. Punto Medio was born precisely from the idea of bringing together different ways of working on our spaces and on ourselves. A project can combine architecture, interior design, landscape design, wellbeing or energy work, as long as it makes sense for what you're going through. Each proposal is built around you, not from predefined packages.

Are the proposals personalized?

Yes. Every person, space and process is different. That's why Punto Medio's proposals start with an initial conversation to understand what you need and tailor the support to your situation, rather than applying a single formula.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the type of project, its scale and its particular needs. Before we begin, we define the different stages, the way we'll work and the estimated timeline for each process.

Inner Space

Reiki, Akashic Records and Hebrew Pendulum.

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. If you know you want to work on something but aren't sure which service can support you, we can talk it through before we begin. The first meeting is precisely for understanding what you're looking for, getting to know your situation and finding the approach that suits you best. You don't need to arrive with a clear answer.

Do these practices replace medical or psychological treatment?

No. The Inner Space practices are offered as tools for wellbeing and personal support, and they do not replace medical or psychological treatment or the advice of health professionals. If you have a health condition, it's important to continue working with the appropriate professional.

Are the workshops in person or online?

It depends on each one. Some experiences can be held online, while others are designed to be shared in person. The format, length and dynamics are described in the information for each workshop or gathering.

How long is a session?

It depends on the service you choose. Each one has its own length and format, which you'll know before we begin.

How do I begin?

You can start by writing to me through the contact form or any of the channels on the website. Tell me briefly what you're looking for, what you'd like to work on or what brings you here. From there we can talk and decide together on the next step.

What happens after I get in touch?

Once I receive your message, we'll set up a first conversation to get to know each other, understand what you're looking for and answer any questions you have. From there we'll decide whether Punto Medio can support you, and how.
