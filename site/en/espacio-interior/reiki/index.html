Reiki

A space to pause, listen to yourself and come back to yourself.

Reiki is a Japanese energy healing technique that helps balance the body, mind and emotions through the laying on of hands.

It doesn't cure directly, but it does support healing and recovery thanks to the pure energy it brings. It's a wonderful complement to other therapies and to everyday life in general.

It's ideal if you're dealing with anxiety, tiredness, insomnia or physical pain, or if you simply want to give yourself a moment of peace and revitalize your energy.

It's an invitation to pause in the middle of everyday life. A moment to slow down, connect with the body and pay attention to what so often stays in the background.

What can a session support?

  • Finding a moment of calm and rest.
  • Releasing tension and slowing down.
  • Connecting with the body and our sensations.
  • Making room for emotions that need to be heard.
  • Supporting moments of change or transformation.
  • Creating a personal space for introspection and self-care.

The experience can be different for each person.
There's no right way to feel a session.

What is a session like?

Initial conversation + chakra reading.

45 minutes of laying on of hands, with or without gentle touch.

Closing and reflecting on the experience. Recommendations to keep your chakras in good balance.

Different elements accompany the therapy, such as music, essential oils and scents, some musical instruments and energy stones.

Reiki for pets 🐾

Reiki can also be done with pets, adapting the session to their needs and respecting their pace and comfort.

The practice is done calmly and respectfully, without forcing contact. It can be done in person or remotely, depending on each case.

When should my pet have Reiki?

When they seem down or stressed, when they're ill, or simply when they're getting very old.
