Recommendations before your session

Before booking, I'll ask for some relevant information to take good care of your experience.

  • Medical or psychological treatments.
  • Medication.
  • Pregnancy.
  • Relevant health conditions.
  • Particular physical or emotional sensitivity.
  • Any other situation you feel is important to share.
  • Scents or elements you may not like.
  • Any questions or fears you may have.

Reiki has no contraindications, but it's always good to know any important details.

Your information is confidential

The information you share will only be used to understand your situation, prepare our session and adapt it to your needs. It will be treated with respect and confidentiality.

An important note

The Inner Space practices are offered as complementary approaches to wellbeing and personal exploration.

They do not replace medical or psychological treatment, or consultation with health professionals.
