Hebrew Pendulum

A tool to explore and harmonize our energy field.

The Hebrew Pendulum is an energy practice that seeks to identify and work with different imbalances in the energy field, using the pendulum and the symbolism of the Hebrew alphabet as tools.

This practice works with different words and codes that help direct, organize and balance energy, addressing aspects related to emotions, relationships, personal growth and the space we inhabit.

It starts from the idea that our energy is constantly in motion, and that certain experiences can create disharmonies that we can observe and work with from an energetic perspective.

When can it support you?

  • When you feel imbalanced or energetically weighed downFeelings of heaviness, disharmony or a need for energy cleansing.
  • When you're going through intense emotionsExploring and working on emotional aspects.
  • In processes of personal growthSelf-knowledge, transformation and reflection.
  • In situations involving relationshipsExploring the energetic side of relationships.
  • In relation to the spaces we inhabitEnergy cleansing and harmonization of your surroundings.
  • When you need to pause and regroupWhen something needs to change, be cleared or find balance.

What is a session like?

Get to know each other

A conversation to find out how you are and what you'd like to explore.

Explore

We define the focus of the session and work on whatever you'd like to look at.

Integrate

We close with time to take in the experience and talk about what came up.

Sessions are held online.

How does it work?

During the session, specific words or codes are chosen according to what is being explored.

The pendulum is used in rotating movements over specific areas of the energy field, to direct the information on the cards and encourage a process of reorganization and balance within the framework of this practice.

The session calls for presence, focus and intuition.
