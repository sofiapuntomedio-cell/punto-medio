Akashic Records

A look at what we're living through.

The Akashic Records are understood as the memory of the soul: a field that holds information about our experiences, thoughts, emotions and lessons. Time doesn't exist there, so past, present and future converge; since time is circular, what we're living now is always a manifestation of what was sown or lived in the past.

They're often pictured as a vast energetic library where the story of every soul is recorded. Accessing them is a way of receiving deep, loving and wise information that can bring new perspectives to our path.

What can we explore?

  • Purpose and personal path.
  • Beliefs and patterns we feel are holding us back.
  • Bonds and relationships.
  • Lessons that may be present in an experience.
  • Questions about what we need to understand right now.
  • Work, projects and vocation.
  • Situations that keep repeating.
  • Processes of change.
  • Personal and spiritual growth.

Sometimes, not knowing exactly what to ask is also part of the process.

What is a session like?

The session takes place online by video call.

We meet

About 10 minutes of conversation.

We open the space

Meditation and channeling, about 45–60 minutes.

We share

A conversation about the information received and any questions that come up.

Before and after the session

Before the session

Prepare your questions in advance and find a quiet, comfortable space where you won't be interrupted. You may also want headphones, soft lighting and a glass of water.

It's important to avoid alcohol and very heavy or stimulating food from the day before the session, to keep our body and energy channel clear.

After the session

Give yourself time to rest and absorb the experience. Drink some water, eat something nice and, if you can, spend time in nature or do something calm.

Practical information

Online sessions. Approximate length: 1 h 15 min to 2 h, including conversation and opening/closing. Payment in advance by bank transfer.
