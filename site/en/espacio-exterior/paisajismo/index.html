Landscape Design

Designing spaces that awaken the senses.

Landscape design transforms patios, gardens, balconies and terraces into spaces that don't just look good, but can also become places to live in, rest, connect and enjoy. I like to design these spaces as sensory experiences, working with different materials, colors, textures and aromatic plants to awaken our senses and bring beauty and health into our lives.

Each proposal starts from the characteristics of the place, the needs of the people who use it and the way we want to experience that space.
