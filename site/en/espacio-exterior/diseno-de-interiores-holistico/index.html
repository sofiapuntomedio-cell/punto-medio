Holistic Interior Design

Transforming our space to transform the way we live in it.

Our spaces are part of our everyday lives. They accompany us and reflect our habits, our needs and our moments of change.

Holistic interior design looks at a space beyond aesthetics, considering how we use it, how we feel in it and what we want it to let us experience.

The intervention can be small or comprehensive: sometimes transforming a single room is enough to make a meaningful change.

What can we work on?

One room

Needs, uses, habits, interests, aesthetics, organization and sensations.

Several rooms

Working on two or more spaces that need to connect or be transformed.

Full service

A global view of an entire home or space.

Each project is approached from an integral perspective, choosing the tools that make sense for each person and each space.

Space as a reflection

Our surroundings can become an extension of our identity, our habits and the moment of life we're in.

Sometimes we're going through an inner change and feel the need to change our surroundings.

Other times, starting by transforming our space can help us make room for something new.

Changing our outer world can also open space to transform our inner one.

How do we work?

Listen

Needs, interests, habits and whatever you'd like to transform.

We don't always need to change everything

Sometimes a transformation doesn't require major construction. Rearranging, removing, adding, moving or giving new meaning to a few elements can completely change how we experience a space.
