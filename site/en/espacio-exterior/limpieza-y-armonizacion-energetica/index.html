Energy Cleansing and Harmonization of Spaces

Renewing the energy of the place we live in.

Spaces go through processes, too.

A move, a breakup, an illness, a stressful period, a renovation or simply the passing of time can make us feel that a place needs renewing.

Energy cleansing and harmonization creates a moment of pause, renewal and intention for the space we inhabit.

It isn't meant to replace physical cleaning or to fix specific problems in the space, but to work on an energetic and symbolic level as a complementary wellbeing practice.

When can it be a good option?

When you move

To clear stagnant energy and energy left by previous occupants, and begin a new chapter with clean, renewed energy.

After construction or a renovation

Supporting change on an energetic level as well. We clear dense and foreign energies.

After an intense period

To create closure and renewal.

When you feel something needs to change

When the atmosphere feels heavy, things keep breaking and you feel stuck. It helps renew the energy of the space and lets it flow better.

Retail spaces

For shops with a lot of foot traffic, when sales are slow, or when you want to give your business a boost.

Medical practices

Spaces that receive people who are ill or low in energy.

Space as a setting for transformation

Our surroundings hold memories, routines, objects, relationships and experiences.

When we transform the way we live in a space, we can also make room for a new chapter.

Sometimes renewing a space is a way of starting over, of giving ourselves the chance to renew ourselves too, to reawaken our drive and energy, and to change our perspective.

When our setting changes, the way we act changes too.

What is the process like?

Getting to know the space

Talking about the place, its history and the reason for the harmonization.
